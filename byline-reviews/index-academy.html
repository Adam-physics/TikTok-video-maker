FAQ

Questions authors ask first.

Are these real people?

Yes. Every review comes from a member of the reader network we work with: people who love to read and sign up to get free books in the genres they enjoy.

Are reviewers paid?

No. Readers receive a free copy of your book and nothing else. They are never paid for a review.

Do readers have to leave a review?

No. After reading, they're invited to share honest feedback. Many choose to post it as an Amazon review. We send your book to enough readers to reach your number.

Will the reviews be positive?

Readers say what they actually think, and we never ask for a rating. That is what makes the reviews credible. Our guarantee covers the number of reviews, not the stars.

Is this allowed by Amazon?

Yes. Amazon's guidelines allow authors to give readers free copies of their book, as long as a review isn't required and the author doesn't try to influence it. That's exactly how this works: readers get a free copy and nothing else, they decide for themselves whether to leave a review, and they write whatever they honestly think. Nobody tells them what to say or what rating to give.

How long does it take?

Reviews usually start arriving within a few weeks. Larger packages take longer because readers need time to read the book. You get a weekly update the whole way.

What do you need from me?

Your Amazon link and your chosen number. We handle the rest.

What books qualify?

Any book that is live on Amazon, in print, Kindle or both.
