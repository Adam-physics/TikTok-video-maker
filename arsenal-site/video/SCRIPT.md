# Arsenal demo video: full script (draft 2)

Runs about 4 minutes, roughly 600 words at Byline's pace (about 150 words a minute), plus musical breaths. It's one continuous camera move with no hard cuts.

**Rail:** a journey rail runs along the bottom the whole time and lights up chapter by chapter: Foundation · Platform · Model · Portfolio · Flywheel · Next · Why now · Why us · Partner.

**Headlines:** each beat gets a short serif headline with one italic word, changing in time with the voice.

**Visuals:** every visual is new and made for this video. Nothing is reused from the website.

**Sources:** every fact comes from arsenalholdingscorp.com. Third-party figures are credited on screen.

---

**Headlines:** Start with an *idea* → Arsenal started with *revenue* → AI-powered. *Human-led.*

**Voice:**

**On screen:**

## 1 · The foundation (0:14–0:58)
**Rail:** Foundation

**Headlines:** No *slide deck* → Energy, *end to end* → Finance for *owners* → The *proving ground*

**Voice:**
> Arsenal didn't start with a slide deck. It started with wells, pipelines and lending desks. Our founders have drilled more than 240 wells.
> In energy, we operate across the value chain: production upstream; pipelines, processing and storage midstream; refined products downstream; and biogas plants that turn waste into energy.
> In finance, we provide growth capital, M&A advisory, and wealth and tax strategy, for energy service companies across North America's main basins.
> These businesses fund everything we build, and they're where our AI proves itself first. In energy, we're deploying agents for maintenance, optimization and procurement. In finance: credit decisions scored on more than fifty industry metrics. What took weeks now takes days.

**On screen:**
- A pitch-deck card gets crossed out.
- A line-art cross-section: well → pipeline → plant → terminal, with product flowing through it. A biogas dome glows green.
- A "240+ wells" counter.
- A "20–50% processing margins on fee-based contracts" chip (Arsenal operations).
- A lending desk: three service cards stack up.
- Agent chips land on the pumpjack and the plant.

## 3 · The studio model (1:10–1:28)
**Rail:** Model

**Headlines:** *Build* → *Embed* → *Scale* → *Compound*, one per beat

**Voice:**

> Build: spin up AI-native companies on the platform.
> Embed: install agentic AI into the operation.

**Rail:** Why now

**Headlines:** The *evidence* → *Transformation* beats efficiency

> Across fifty-one studied deployments, agentic AI delivered a seventy-one percent median productivity gain.
