§ 01 The Thesis

We don’t just fund companies.
We build them, and we run them.

Most venture studios start with an idea and go looking for revenue. Arsenal started the other way around. Its foundation is a set of operating businesses in energy production, midstream infrastructure, waste-to-energy and specialized energy finance, and those businesses produce cash flow today.

That base pays for a second act. On one shared AI platform, Arsenal builds and acquires vertical-market businesses in accounting, insurance and wealth, then puts AI and agentic services into what those businesses sell. Every new company inherits the same tooling, the same data discipline and the same operators.

The result is a portfolio meant to compound: hard assets underneath, software leverage on top, and people who are accountable for both.

§ 03 The Model

A studio with a
balance sheet.

  1. I.

    Operate the foundation

    Energy production, infrastructure and waste-to-energy conversion, plus specialized financing and M&A for energy service companies across North America’s main basins.

  2. II.

    Share one AI platform

    The same models, data pipelines and agentic services run underneath every portfolio company, so each new company starts with what the last one built.

  3. III.

    Acquire or partner with proven businesses

    Arsenal buys or partners with vertical-market businesses that already have customers, then builds AI into the products they sell.

  4. IV.

    Run them with people

    The AI does the work it is good at, and named operators are accountable for the results.
