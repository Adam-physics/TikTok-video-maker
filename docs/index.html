Questions

What people ask before the call.

Are the reviews real?

Yes. Readers in our advance reader network get a free copy and leave their own honest review. Nobody is paid for a rating and we never write reviews ourselves, which keeps your listing within Amazon's rules. We can't promise a star rating. We can promise a book that deserves a good one.

Do I need a book idea?

No. We research your field and pitch you topics with working titles. If you already have an idea, we'll shape the book around it.

How much do I need to do?

Very little. You answer questions on our site by typing or voice note, then read and approve. No interviews or meetings are required. We handle the rest.

Will it sound like me?

We write from your answers and voice notes, so the ideas and the way you explain them are yours. You read every chapter and we revise until you're happy.

Who owns the book?

You do. Copyright, royalties and the Amazon listing are all in your name.

Can the book be about me?

Most clients choose an expert book on a topic in their field. If you'd rather have a memoir or biography, our personal story edition is available. It takes much more of your input and costs more, so we'll talk it through on the call.

Will anyone know you wrote it?

No. Everything is confidential and we never name a client without permission.
